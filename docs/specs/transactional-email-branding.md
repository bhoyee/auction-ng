# Transactional email branding and collection disclosures

Owner request: 2026-11-08. Use the actual LotVanta logo, a shared indigo/yellow banner and the existing design tokens throughout transactional email: authentication/security codes, winner/Buy Now receipts, outbid/watch updates, pre-bids, cancellations, withdrawals, refunds, pickup notices, support and staff alerts.

`packages/ui/emails/brand.ts` generates the shared header. Its optimised PNG is a 16 KB derivative of the approved website logo. `pnpm tokens:generate` regenerates the header/logo transport asset and all shared email templates. SMTP sends the static PNG as a CID attachment; local Mailpit preview embeds the same image as a data URI. No external image, tracking pixel or font download is required. HTML retains a LotVanta alt-text fallback and each message keeps its plain-text alternative. Updating the brand asset requires updating the optimised PNG and regenerating templates.

Winner and fixed-price receipt delivery reads the buyer-owned invoice line's recorded pickup case using the existing invoice-number/line/lot indexes. A saved case supplies its current WAT deadline, snapshotted daily rate/cap, waiver and collection status. Current centre settings and purchase dates are not substituted for those saved terms. Payment deadlines remain a separate field. This is a read-only disclosure; ledger operations and storage calculations are unchanged.

The approved policy is five full centre working days after the item is paid, marked physically ready and the buyer is notified. The saved calendar excludes centre closed days and dates; approved extensions remain reflected in the recorded deadline. A receipt sent before readiness explains the five-working-day window and says the exact deadline/rate/cap will follow in the readiness notice. It does not invent a date or silently start the window. Ready receipts show the exact WAT deadline and item-specific charges. Waived items do not threaten new charges; collected items do not ask the buyer to collect again. Storage is paid explicitly at pickup, with no automatic wallet debit. The initial rate/cap remain ₦1,000 per centre working day / ₦10,000 per item, configurable through the existing authorised controls. Contact/review remains distinct from forfeiture.

Focused template, escaping, source-generation, saved-policy ownership, SMTP attachment/retry and local-preview tests cover the changes. Winner and authentication previews are captured at 360/768/1280px under `docs/design/email-branding/`; all data/codes in those previews are synthetic. SMTP tests use a mock transport and do not claim delivery to a real inbox. Existing delivered messages are unchanged; subsequent deliveries use the revised templates.
